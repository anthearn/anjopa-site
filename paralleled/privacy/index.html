Paralleled privacy & support

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Paralleled is made by AnJoPa, the gaming brand of AJP TECHNICAL LIMITED (UK company number 06338622). We are responsible for the personal information we handle as described in this notice.

For support or privacy questions, email info@ajptechnical.com.

Internal testing: some builds show simulated advertising only; Google test-ad builds also use Google's advertising and privacy SDKs. Purchase screens in these internal builds are simulations: they do not charge you. This notice will be reviewed before real advertising or payments are introduced.

A short explanation for players and parents

You do not need to create a game account. Your scores, collected elements, Fish Coins and settings are saved on your device. Google test ads need an internet connection and can send technical information to Google. We configure these ads for family-friendly content and do not request personalized advertising.

Game progress and choices

The game stores progress, scores, achievements, coins, difficulty, controls, sound, language and test-purchase status locally. This is used to provide the game and remember your choices; we do not currently run an online leaderboard, player-account service or our own cloud-save server.

Google test-ad builds offer a coarse age choice: under 13, 13–17, 18 or over, or prefer not to say. We do not ask for your date of birth. The selected group is stored on your device; appropriate age-treatment signals are supplied to Google's privacy services. Ads remain disabled if no age choice is supplied. You can revisit this through Settings → Info → Privacy & age in those builds.

Advertising and Google's services

Builds using Google's advertising SDK communicate with Google to load and display test advertisements, obtain applicable privacy choices, and support ad security and operation. Google's SDK documentation describes processing of IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), interactions such as ad views and taps, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers depending on the SDK configuration and available permissions.

Our Google test-ad profile requests G-rated, child-safe, non-personalized ads for all ages, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier, and removes Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions from the Android manifest. Non-personalized advertising still involves technical data processing: it does not mean that no information is sent to Google. We do not request GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone access for advertising.

Where required, Google's consent process is completed before ad requests. You can revisit available Google privacy controls using Review ad privacy in the game's Privacy & age screen. If privacy checks fail, the game remains playable without loading ads. Declining a rewarded continue does not spend its points; you can finish the run normally.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures. Google's processing and retention are described in its notices; we do not control all data held by Google.

Children and families

Paralleled is intended to provide a family-friendly experience. We do not request personalized advertising or remarketing, and use conservative content and privacy settings across age groups. We do not ask children to provide a name, email, full birthday or game account to play. A parent or guardian can contact us with questions about the game or its handling of information.

Advertising settings and age guidance are reviewed separately from the game's store content rating. Test advertising is not a substitute for checking the final family-safe advertising configuration before public release.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate a problem or handle a privacy request. Our email service provider processes messages on our behalf. We do not need your password, payment-card details or identity documents for ordinary game support.

Why information is used

Where UK or EEA data-protection law applies, we use information needed to provide the service you request, to pursue legitimate interests such as responding to support enquiries and keeping the service secure, to meet legal obligations where applicable, and with consent where required for advertising-related technologies. You can withdraw an available consent choice through the game's privacy controls. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Storage, retention and international processing

Local game data normally stays until you clear the app or browser storage or uninstall the game. Your operating system or browser backup settings may retain or restore copies. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete local game progress that has not been sent to us.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to answer the enquiry, resolve a problem and meet applicable obligations. Google and other service providers may process information in countries outside your own; their notices describe applicable transfer safeguards and retention practices. Contact us if you need more information about a particular request.

Your choices and rights

You can change the game's saved settings, revisit available privacy choices, or clear its local storage. Clearing storage can erase progress. Depending on your location and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent.

To exercise a right over information we hold, contact info@ajptechnical.com. Where a provider holds information independently, its own privacy controls or request process may also be relevant. In the UK you can raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office; elsewhere you can contact your local data-protection authority.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when the game's data practices change, including before introducing real purchases, accounts, online scores or a materially different advertising setup.
